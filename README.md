### ALGO-000036: Majority outside ACC⁰
- **Subfield:** Boolean circuit and formula complexity
- **Open:** Prove that majority cannot be computed by polynomial-size ACC⁰ circuits, allowing any fixed modulus and fixed depth.
- **Status:** Majority is outside AC⁰, but its separation from ACC⁰ remains unresolved. This is a concrete function-specific target rather than a class-separation formulation.
- **Type:** impossibility
- **Why:** Would separate approximate counting power from constant-depth modular computation.
- **Key works:** Razborov and Smolensky’s 1980s modular-circuit lower bounds; Williams (2011), *Non-uniform ACC Circuit Lower Bounds*; Hoza’s course.
- **Related:** Neural computation—threshold versus modular-gate expressiveness.
